## Who they are

Scientific Biotech Specialties, Inc. (SBSI) is a Philippine distributor of in vitro diagnostic (IVD) products — lab instruments, reagents, consumables, and spare parts — along with training, technical support, and IT solutions for hospitals and clinical laboratories nationwide. It also distributes manufacturing instrumentation, controls, and quality-assurance solutions to industrial laboratories in the pharmaceutical, food, and beverage sectors ([source](https://sbsi.com.ph/aboutus/)).

SBSI was founded in 1998 and has been operating in the Philippine diagnostics market for over 25 years. It is currently the market leader for electrophoresis testing in the Philippines and ranks among the top three players in clinical hematology and immunohematology.

## Footprint

Headquartered in Makati City, Metro Manila, with the main warehouse and after-sales operations covering North Luzon, South Luzon, and the National Capital Region. Regional operations extend to Cebu City (Visayas, with a warehouse in Iloilo City) and Davao City / Cagayan de Oro City (Mindanao, with a warehouse in Zamboanga City).
